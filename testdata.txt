DECISION CRITERIA SUMMARY (INDIA - INR)
================================================================================

APPROVE Cases:
  ✓ Credit Score: >= 750
  ✓ Monthly Liability Ratio: < 30% of monthly income
  ✓ Loan Tenure: Suitable for income level
  ✓ Employment: Stable sector (2+ years minimum)

REVIEW Cases:
  ⚠ Credit Score: 650-749 (Marginal)
  ⚠ Monthly Liability Ratio: 30-40% (Borderline)
  ⚠ Employment: Recent changes or self-employed
  ⚠ Multiple loan purposes or significant liability increase

REJECT Cases:
  ✗ Credit Score: < 650 (Poor)
  ✗ Monthly Liability Ratio: > 40% (Excessive)
  ✗ Unstable or insufficient employment history
  ✗ Suspicious credit behavior or defaults
